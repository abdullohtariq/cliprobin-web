Privacy Policy

Clip Robin processes video inspection, audio extraction, faster-whisper transcription, YuNet ONNX face detection, and FFmpeg rendering locally on your Windows PC. Raw video files are not uploaded to Clip Robin servers.

When you opt into AI Hook Finder, timestamped transcript text is sent to your configured AI provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or DeepSeek). When you connect YouTube Shorts publishing, OAuth2 credentials are used solely to upload the clips you select.
